# Phase32-I — Cash Frontier Final Causality / NO_VALID_COMPETITOR Deep Audit

## Executive Summary

This audit is read-only. No production code, configuration, thresholds, model logic, PM/PC/MCC/Risk Pacing/PS/Runtime behavior, or architecture implementation was changed.

The Healthy-BULL cash frontier is not explained by a hard cash ceiling or by missing candidate supply. It is explained by a final deployability rule: a NEW/ADD candidate must enter PC capital competition as `COMPETITOR_SELECTED` with positive `accepted_weight` before MCC can let it defeat Cash. `COMPARABLE_HIGH` and `STRONG` describe opportunity quality; they do not by themselves create deployable capital authority.

For the five focus dates, all candidates on 2024-01-11, 2024-01-15, and 2024-01-23 had `accepted_weight = 0` and `status = COMPETITOR_REJECTED_RECONSIDERABLE`; MCC therefore converted otherwise `NORMAL_*_DEPLOY` quality into `FAIL_CLOSED` via `competitor_not_selected_by_current_pc_context`, leaving Cash as the only selected final competitor. On 2024-01-24 and 2024-01-31 one NEW candidate survived as `COMPETITOR_SELECTED`, won the single MCC path, and Cash remained materially as residual capital.

## NO_VALID_COMPETITOR Causality

`NO_VALID_COMPETITOR` is not a statement that no buy-quality candidates existed. It means no deployable security competitor was valid in the final capital-competition context.

The code path is:

1. Build competitors from members.
2. Compute `requested_weight` and `accepted_weight`.
3. Set `selected = accepted_weight > tolerance or symbol in selected_symbols`.
4. If not selected, emit `COMPETITOR_REJECTED_RECONSIDERABLE` or terminal rejection plus a constraint reason such as `REENTRY_BLOCK` or `REALLOCATABLE_RESIDUAL_PENDING_RECONSIDERATION`.
5. Build cash evidence and count deployable securities as `status == COMPETITOR_SELECTED`.
6. If lot/residual evidence says `NO_ELIGIBLE_OPPORTUNITY` or `COMPETITION_EXHAUSTED`, cash reason codes include `NO_VALID_COMPETITOR`.
7. MCC evaluates quality/risk. Under `NORMAL_DEPLOYMENT`, these candidates would map to `DEPLOY_ELIGIBLE`, but non-selected status overrides them to `FAIL_CLOSED`.
8. If no security interaction result remains `DEPLOY_ELIGIBLE` or `SELECTIVE_COMPETITION`, Cash wins.

The decisive field chain on cash-win focus dates is:

`target_weight/requested_weight/accepted_weight = 0` -> `status = COMPETITOR_REJECTED_RECONSIDERABLE` -> `current_competitor_status != COMPETITOR_SELECTED` -> `interaction_result = FAIL_CLOSED` -> `available_deployable_competitor_count = 0` -> `capital_competition_winner_type = CASH_OPTIONALITY`.

## MARGINAL_OPPORTUNITY_SET Causality

`MARGINAL_OPPORTUNITY_SET` is generated inside the Cash semantic layer from the canonical opportunity-quality distribution. It is not a direct score threshold, not a market-quality threshold, and not a Cash-vs-security price comparison.

The condition is: marginal-quality securities exist and no `STRONG` security exists. In code, `COMPARABLE_MARGINAL + WEAK_VALID > 0` and `STRONG == 0` appends `MARGINAL_OPPORTUNITY_SET`. Under `NORMAL_DEPLOYMENT`, this returns `OPTIONALITY_NEUTRAL` plus `HEALTHY_MARKET_OPTIONALITY_LOW`.

Therefore:

- 2024-01-11 and 2024-01-15 carry `MARGINAL_OPPORTUNITY_SET` because they contain `COMPARABLE_MARGINAL` candidates and no `STRONG`.
- 2024-01-23 does not carry `MARGINAL_OPPORTUNITY_SET`; it carries `STRONG_OPPORTUNITY_PRESENT` because 15140 is `STRONG`.
- 2024-01-24 and 2024-01-31 have deployable selected NEW winners, so their residual cash is `COMPETITION_EXHAUSTED`, not pure no-deployable cash.

## Cash Competitor Semantic

Cash is active as a competitor record and passive as residual capital. The focus period is therefore mixed.

Active aspects:

- Cash is explicitly inserted into the `competitor_set`.
- Cash has `competitor_type = CASH_OPTIONALITY`.
- Cash has `current_competitor_status = COMPETITOR_SELECTED`.
- Cash has `interaction_result = SELECTIVE_COMPETITION`.
- Cash consumes canonical cash evidence, risk pacing, and opportunity quality.

Passive aspects:

- Cash does not score alpha or recompute market quality/risk pacing.
- Cash does not directly beat a selected deployable security under `NORMAL_DEPLOYMENT`; selected securities win first.
- On 2024-01-24 and 2024-01-31, Cash remains because security deployment did not consume all available capital.

## COMPARABLE_HIGH Paradox

The paradox is explained.

`COMPARABLE_HIGH` is an opportunity-quality class, not an allocation authorization. The focus rows show `COMPARABLE_HIGH` candidates with `BUY_NEW_ALLOWED`, but still `requested_weight = 0`, `accepted_weight = 0`, and `COMPETITOR_REJECTED_RECONSIDERABLE`. MCC initially maps `COMPARABLE_HIGH` under `NORMAL_DEPLOYMENT` to deployable, then fail-closes because the candidate was not selected by PC context.

Concrete examples:

- 2024-01-15: 83060, 24680, and 54010 are all `COMPARABLE_HIGH`, all `BUY_NEW_ALLOWED`, all `REENTRY_BLOCK`, all `FAIL_CLOSED`, all allocation/fill zero.
- 2024-01-31: 83060 is `COMPARABLE_HIGH` and `FULL_ALLOCATION_ELIGIBLE`, but PC accepted weight is zero and MCC fail-closes it. The shadow multi-allocation view gives 0.031250, but the shadow artifact has `authoritative_consumer_count = 0` and `trading_consumer_connected = false`.

## Compounded Conservatism

The focus period shows compounded conservatism, but not a proven mandatory defect:

- Entry admission often permits only reduced allocation.
- Many candidates are reentry-blocked.
- Several candidates are residual/reconsideration candidates rather than first-pass selected competitors.
- MCC has a selected-status safety override, so even NORMAL deployable quality cannot compete without accepted weight.
- Cash is modeled as first-class optionality and remains selected with full residual weight.
- The shadow multi-allocation path can express partial security allocation, but it is non-authoritative for trading in the inspected artifacts.

This is a capital-frontier resolution limitation and calibration question: the system can describe marginal opportunities but the authoritative single path has coarse admission from candidate quality into actual capital deployment.

## Defect / Limitation Classification

No mandatory defect is established. The implementation follows its current contract:

- PM/PC/PS ownership is preserved.
- Risk Pacing is consumed, not recomputed, in MCC.
- Cash is first-class.
- Runtime is not redeciding the PC/MCC winner.
- Future/outcome evidence is not used by the inspected quality and cash paths.

The limitation is architectural and calibration-oriented: the system lacks an authoritative, high-resolution bridge from “candidate is quality-valid and reduced-admissible” to “candidate receives small positive capital before Cash absorbs residual.” Shadow artifacts already point toward such a bridge, but they are not implementation-ready as production authority.

## Repair Readiness

Production repair is not justified now. The observed issue is not a wrong field, wrong denominator, contract mismatch, stale authority, future leakage, or unintended duplicate capital authority. The next change should remain shadow-first and should focus on evidence observability:

- produce a candidate-level `quality_to_capital_admission_bridge` table for every NEW/ADD competitor;
- explicitly record why `requested_weight` and `accepted_weight` are zero despite `BUY_NEW_ALLOWED`, `COMPARABLE_HIGH`, or `STRONG`;
- compare that bridge against low-cash positive controls before changing any production gate.
