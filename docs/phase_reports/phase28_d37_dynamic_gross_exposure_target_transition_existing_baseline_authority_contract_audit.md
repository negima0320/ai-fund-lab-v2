## Authority Matrix

```text
Market Context:
  May signal risk. May not set orders or sell symbols.

Portfolio Policy:
  May set target_gross_exposure. May not directly sell or choose sell symbols.

Position Management:
  May emit HOLD / ADD / REDUCE / EXIT for existing positions.
  Current implementation does not convert aggregate target shortfall into de-risk.

Portfolio Construction:
  May allocate target weights and execute PM REDUCE / EXIT target semantics.
  May not override HOLD / ADD into REDUCE / EXIT.

Position Sizing:
  May size quantity deltas from target weights.
  May not create new sell authority.

Runtime Planning:
  May materialize orders from quantity deltas.
  May not create new sell authority.

Safety:
  May block/halt. Ordinary de-risk symbol selection is not its current authority.
```

## Over-Target Contract Decision

Current and recommended contract split:

```text

PM EXIT while over target = EXECUTE
```
