# Phase6-K Expanded Random Historical Validation

## 1. Purpose

Phase6-K expands the Phase6-J end-to-end smoke test from one random date per year to five random business dates per year.

Scope:

```text
Phase4 Candidate AI artifact
-> Candidate Top50
-> Phase5 Opportunity AI re-scoring
-> Top3 / Top5 / Top10 buy-candidate validation
-> future outcome check for evaluation only
```

This is not a trading simulation and does not execute Broker API, orders, Paper Trading, Capital Allocation, live orders, or real account updates.

Completion decision:

```text
Risk Guard is conservative and skipped high-volatility names.
```

The skipped group has lower positive rate and worse downside, but higher mean future return and much higher max return due to upside outliers. Risk Guard is useful as a downside / win-rate filter, but it may skip lottery-like winners. This should be calibrated carefully instead of treated as purely successful.

## 14. Consideration

Phase6-K confirms that the Phase4 -> Phase5 buy-candidate pipeline is not merely succeeding on one lucky date per year. Top3 and Top5 outperform Top10, and tail dilution is reconfirmed.

However, the result includes meaningful findings:

- Top3 is stronger than Top5.
- Top4-5 is weak in this sample.
- Risk Guard skips high-volatility names with worse downside and lower win rate, but also skips upside outliers.
- 2026 remains weak and should be treated as a regime-sensitivity warning.
